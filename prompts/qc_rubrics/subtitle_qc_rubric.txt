# Subtitle QC Rubric — Korean → {target_language}
# Used by the QA agent to score and flag each translated segment

## Scoring dimensions (each 0.0–1.0, equal weight)

### 1. Cultural accuracy (weight: 0.35)
SEVERE (score < 0.4):
  - Idiom or honorific translated literally, producing nonsense or offense
  - Cultural concept left untranslated with no adaptation
  - Food, place, or cultural reference creates factual confusion for a {target_language}-speaking audience

MODERATE (score 0.4–0.7):
  - Cultural adaptation present but awkward or slightly off-register
  - Honorific handling inconsistent with earlier segments
  - Humor adaptation attempts but misses the comic intent

MINOR (score 0.7–0.9):
  - Stylistic preference — adaptation works but a better one exists
  - Slightly formal/casual mismatch

PASS (score >= 0.9):
  - All cultural references handled appropriately for a {target_language}-speaking audience

### 2. Meaning accuracy (weight: 0.30)
SEVERE: Meaning is reversed, a key plot point is wrong, or a name is wrong
MODERATE: A meaningful detail is omitted or changed
MINOR: Minor word choice difference that does not affect meaning
PASS: Meaning fully preserved

### 3. Line formatting (weight: 0.20)
SEVERE: Subtitle exceeds 2 lines or any line exceeds 60 characters
MODERATE: Line break splits a grammatical unit awkwardly (e.g. splits a phrasal verb)
MINOR: Punctuation style inconsistent with earlier segments
PASS: Within formatting constraints, natural line breaks

### 4. Tone and register (weight: 0.15)
SEVERE: Formal speech rendered as profanity or vice versa
MODERATE: Register mismatch with original (e.g. casual original → stiff {target_language})
MINOR: Slightly unnatural phrasing for the character's voice
PASS: Register and tone match original

## Overall score formula
overall = (cultural * 0.35) + (meaning * 0.30) + (formatting * 0.20) + (tone * 0.15)

## Pass threshold
overall >= 0.80 → PASS (no further iteration needed for this segment)
overall < 0.80 → FAIL (send fix_hints back to translation agent)

## fix_hint format
Each issue must include a concrete, actionable fix_hint:
BAD:  "The translation is culturally inappropriate"
GOOD: "눈치 is translated as 'instinct' — change to 'reading the room' or 'social awareness' to preserve the cultural meaning"

BAD:  "Line too long"
GOOD: "Line 1 is 51 characters. Break after 'yesterday' to put the dependent clause on line 2"
